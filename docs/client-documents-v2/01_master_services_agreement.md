This Master Services Agreement (MSA) governs the software services specified in a Statement of Work (SOW) signed by both parties. Each SOW must identify this MSA. A proposal is non-binding unless its provisions are expressly incorporated in a signed SOW. No project begins before its SOW is effective and its start conditions are satisfied.

Any consents, approvals or signing formalities required by applicable law must be obtained before the relevant agreement becomes effective. A representative signing for a party must identify that capacity and have authority to sign.

The parties contract independently. Neither may bind the other or claim an employment, partnership or agency relationship merely because of these services, subject to mandatory law.

A later signed Change Order controls the particular SOW items it expressly changes. A SOW overrides an MSA clause only if it identifies the clause and states the agreed replacement. A Care & Support Agreement governs its separate support scope. Other terms remain unchanged. An invoice or routine message does not unilaterally amend an agreement.

## 2. Scope, client inputs and changes
The Provider will perform the deliverables, milestones and tests expressly listed in the SOW with reasonable care and skill. Unless expressly included, services exclude hosting, domains, paid software or API subscriptions, content creation, legal compliance audits, security certification and ongoing operation or support.

The Client will supply lawful content, necessary permissions, accurate instructions, agreed access and timely consolidated feedback. The Client remains responsible for its business decisions and approval of its content. Each party will promptly flag a dependency or risk affecting performance. The Provider may refuse or suspend unlawful work, notifying the Client where lawful.

The SOW identifies one approval contact for routine decisions. Unless the parties agree otherwise in the SOW, changes to price, deliverables, deadlines or ongoing obligations require a Change Order signed by authorized parties. A chat request alone does not authorize additional billable work.

## 3. Timing and revisions
Dates depend on receipt of the agreed advance, materials, access and approvals. The Provider will promptly explain any expected delay, its cause and a reasonable revised schedule. A delay does not by itself authorize additional charges. Any charge or material scope adjustment requires written agreement.

Unless the SOW states otherwise, the project includes two consolidated revision rounds within its approved scope. A round is one coordinated list of requested refinements to the current deliverable. Correcting a failure to meet an agreed acceptance criterion is not a revision round and does not consume the allowance. New features or changed requirements are handled through a Change Order.

## 4. Price, invoices and payments
The SOW sets the fixed price, currency and milestone allocation. Default terms, unless replaced in the SOW, are USD, 50% advance to start and 50% after express acceptance but before final handover. An invoice is due seven calendar days after issue, subject to any later date printed on it. The Provider must not invoice the final milestone before the agreed acceptance trigger.

Payment is by bank transfer to a bank-verified account held in the Provider's legal name. An invoice must state its number, the relevant SOW and milestone, amount, due date and verified payment instructions. Never send card security codes or account credentials. The parties will independently verify a change of bank details using a previously trusted contact channel.

Unless the SOW expressly allocates fees differently, the Client pays its sending-bank fee and the Provider bears receiving or intermediary fees, with no automatic top-up. Bank deductions must be reconciled against the remittance evidence. Statutory withholding is not a bank fee: the Client may deduct withholding required by law, must provide the available official evidence promptly, and must notify the Provider in advance where practicable. No tax gross-up applies unless expressly agreed and lawful.

Verified deductions for fees borne by the Provider and legally required withholding count toward settlement for handover and rights-transfer purposes; they are recorded separately from cash actually received.

The SOW must specify applicable tax treatment. Neither party is promised a tax exemption. Each party is responsible for its own taxes as required by law. An advance invoice is a request for payment, not proof of payment. A payment is recorded only when cleared or otherwise verified. Prior amounts already invoiced must not be invoiced again; an unpaid prior invoice remains separately outstanding.

The Client should identify any invoice dispute promptly with reasons. The parties will seek to reconcile it, and undisputed amounts remain payable. After written notice and five calendar days to remedy an overdue undisputed amount, the Provider may suspend affected work. The Provider will explain the schedule impact; suspension does not create an unagreed late fee or authorize deletion of Client data.

## 5. Review and express acceptance
The Provider will submit the review deliverables, test instructions and a delivery notice to the SOW contact. Unless the SOW changes it, the Client has seven calendar days to test against the agreed criteria and either accept, accept with a specifically agreed list of minor items and completion dates, or identify material failures with reproducible details.

Silence, an invoice, a payment or a bare delivery link is not acceptance. If feedback is late, the Provider may send a reminder and pause dependent work after notice, but must not mark the project accepted automatically. For a reported failure within scope, the Provider will correct it within a reasonable agreed period and resubmit it for review. New preferences or features are change requests.

Acceptance must identify the SOW and delivered version through a signed confirmation or an express email / recorded portal decision by the authorized contact. A portal event is evidence of that decision; it is not represented as a qualified electronic signature. A disputed material failure must be resolved or expressly excluded by signed agreement before final acceptance.

## 6. Intellectual property and final handover
"Project Work" means the project-specific deliverables expressly identified for transfer in the SOW, excluding Background Materials and Third-Party Materials. To the extent the Provider owns and may lawfully transfer them, the Provider hereby assigns to the Client the worldwide economic copyright and other assignable intellectual property rights in the Project Work, effective only upon full cleared payment of the price attributable to that Project Work and any required signed transfer formalities. Non-transferable rights remain subject to applicable law.

"Background Materials" are the Provider's pre-existing or independently developed tools, libraries, templates and general know-how, identified in the SOW or handover inventory. The Provider retains them. After the payment condition above, the Provider grants the Client a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use, copy, modify, maintain and distribute Background Materials incorporated in the paid deliverables as needed to use or exploit those deliverables, including through the Client's contractors and successors. This does not license unrelated standalone products.

"Third-Party Materials" remain subject to their owners' licenses, not this assignment. The Provider will disclose known material license conditions, recurring costs and any proposed component requiring disclosure of Client proprietary code before inclusion, obtain approval for such obligations, and include an inventory at handover. The Provider must secure any contributor permissions needed for the rights promised here. No blanket "work made for hire" characterization is made.

Before full payment, the Client may use review copies only to evaluate the deliverables unless the SOW expressly permits production use. Client-supplied content remains the Client's property; the Provider receives only permission needed to perform the services. Final handover follows acceptance, payment and the SOW checklist. Access secrets must be transferred through an agreed secure channel, not placed in this document.

## 7. Confidentiality, privacy and publicity
Each party will protect the other's non-public business information with reasonable care, use it only for this relationship and share it only with persons who need it and are bound to appropriate confidentiality duties. This excludes information already lawfully known, independently developed, public without breach, or lawfully received without restriction. Legally required disclosure is permitted, with prior notice where lawful. These duties continue for three years after termination; trade secrets remain protected while qualifying under applicable law.

On completion or termination, the parties will return or delete confidential materials reasonably requested, except records required by law or a limited archive necessary to evidence the work or defend claims. Archives must be access-restricted, retained only as long as justified and not used for a new project. No public repository, portfolio display, Client name, logo, screenshot or testimonial may be used without the Client's express written approval identifying what may be published.

Before access to personal data or regulated information, the parties must agree necessary processing instructions, security measures and any legally required data-processing terms. The Client must not provide unnecessary sensitive data. Each party will notify the other without undue delay of a known incident affecting the other's data handled under the services, cooperate reasonably and comply with applicable legal notification requirements.

## 8. Defect correction and support
For fourteen calendar days after express acceptance, unless the SOW provides a longer period, the Provider will correct reproducible defects reported during that period that cause the accepted work to fail an agreed criterion, at no additional charge. A timely report remains eligible after the period ends. Agreed minor items on the acceptance record remain due on their stated dates. This remedy does not reduce mandatory legal rights.

The correction obligation does not cover new features or failures to the extent caused by Client modifications, misuse, changed third-party services or environments outside the SOW. The Provider will explain the basis of an exclusion. No uninterrupted availability, commercial outcome or issue-free software is promised. Hosting, updates, monitoring, backups and continuing support exist only if expressly purchased under a SOW or Care & Support Agreement.

## 9. Cancellation and termination
The Client may cancel a SOW by written notice. Either party may terminate for a material breach not cured within ten calendar days after written notice describing it, or immediately where continuation would be unlawful. An affected deadline may be reasonably extended for events outside a party's reasonable control after prompt notice and mitigation; if the disruption lasts thirty calendar days, either party may end the affected SOW by notice.

On termination, the Provider will give an itemized closing statement for work actually performed at the SOW's agreed milestone allocation or cancellation rate, plus approved, unavoidable third-party commitments, without double counting or charging unearned profit. If no valuation method was agreed, the parties must agree a reasonable allocation; the Provider cannot set a new rate unilaterally. An advance is not automatically non-refundable.

The Provider will return the unused advance within fourteen calendar days after the closing balance is agreed, subject to mandatory law, and will promptly refund any undisputed unused amount. The Client will pay any undisputed additional earned balance under the normal invoice terms. The Provider will deliver paid-for completed or usable partial work and Client materials; the applicable ownership and license provisions continue for that paid work. If the Provider cancels without Client breach, the same accounting and refund rules apply, without limiting the Client's other rights.

## 10. Responsibility, law and notices
Each party is responsible for direct loss it causes by breach, subject to applicable law and reasonable mitigation. Except for fraud, intentional misconduct, gross negligence, unpaid agreed fees or liability that cannot lawfully be limited, each party's aggregate contractual liability relating to a SOW is limited to the total price payable under that SOW. The parties do not exclude liability that mandatory law preserves. This clause requires jurisdiction-specific review before use.

The law of the Republic of Moldova governs, without removing mandatory protections that apply to either party. The parties will first seek a good-faith resolution within thirty calendar days after a dispute notice, without preventing urgent protective relief. No exclusive court or arbitration forum is selected by this template; proceedings may be brought before a court having jurisdiction under applicable rules.

Notices go to the party emails above, with reasonable evidence of transmission and a follow-up if delivery fails. Signed counterparts and electronic signatures may be used to the extent valid under applicable law; a typed name alone is not assumed to meet every formality. The MSA and its signed SOWs / changes form the agreement for their subject matter. If a term is unenforceable, the remaining terms continue as far as lawful. Required consents or representative documents must accompany execution where applicable.
